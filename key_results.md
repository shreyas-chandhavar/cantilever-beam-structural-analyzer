# Key Results

## Baseline design

- Beam: 1.5 m cantilever, 50 mm × 100 mm rectangular section
- Material properties used: E = 69 GPa, yield strength = 276 MPa
- End point load: 2.5 kN
- Maximum bending stress: 45.00 MPa
- Maximum deflection: 9.78 mm
- Yield factor of safety: 6.13
- Maximum strain: 652.17 microstrain
- Maximum slope: 0.00978 rad = 0.5605°
- Yield load: 15.33 kN
- Deflection-based load limit (5 mm): 1.278 kN
- Stress utilization: 16.30%
- Deflection utilization: 195.65%

## Design conclusion

The original beam is safe against yielding but fails the 5 mm serviceability requirement. Deflection/stiffness governs the design.

## Required sizing and redesign

- Height required by strength: 40.38 mm
- Height required by deflection: 125.07 mm
- Governing analytical height: 125.07 mm
- First feasible height in 50-point parametric search: 126.12 mm
- Redesigned stress: 28.77 MPa
- Redesigned deflection: 5.00 mm
- Redesigned factor of safety: 9.59
- Height increase: 25.07%
- Stress reduction: 36.07%
- Deflection reduction: 48.89%
